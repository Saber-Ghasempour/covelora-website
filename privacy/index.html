Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version. Your game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Covelora does not require a player account. Google’s advertising services process certain device, interaction, and diagnostic data. You can contact us about privacy at covelora@afsanora.com.

This policy explains how Afsanora (“we”, “us”) handles information in Covelora, including its Android test builds (package com.afsanora.covelora), and on covelora.afsanora.com. It describes the current game, rather than features that may be added later.

1. Information we handle

Game progress and settings on your device

Covelora saves level progress, coins, stars, lives and regeneration times, win streaks, boosters, restoration and decoration choices, story progress, and audio, vibration, and appearance settings locally. These records let the game resume your progress and remember your preferences. The current game has no Afsanora player account, cloud-save service, or server-hosted player profile. We do not receive a copy of your local save through the game.

Your operating system may back up or restore app data according to your device and account settings. Such backups are controlled by the platform provider and your settings, not by an Afsanora cloud-save service.

Advertising and consent information

Covelora integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and the ads served, Google’s services process information such as:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and applicable identifiers related to signed-in accounts;
  • app and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, hangs, and energy usage; and
  • privacy choices and consent status used to determine whether and how ads may be requested.

These services use information to deliver and measure advertising, perform analytics, prevent fraud, and maintain their services. The current test build uses Google’s test ad identifiers. Test ads do not mean that the SDK makes no network requests or processes no information.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any screenshots, device details, or other material you choose to send. We use this information to answer questions, investigate bugs, and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or information unrelated to your request.

Information we do not request

The current game does not ask you to provide your real name, date of birth, contacts, precise GPS location, camera access, or microphone recordings. It does not include player-to-player chat. The current build does not include real-money purchasing or collect payment-card details; purchases made with game coins use your in-game balance.

2. Advertising and privacy choices

Rewarded ads are offered for optional in-game benefits. Watching one is your choice. However, the SDK may initialize and load ads before you choose to watch one, subject to the applicable consent checks. Declining to watch an ad is therefore not the same as opting out of all SDK data processing.

The game uses Google’s consent flow where applicable before requesting ads. When Google requires an entry point for changing privacy choices, the game displays a Privacy control that reopens those options. The options available depend on your region and Google’s configuration. Non-personalized or limited advertising may still involve data processing for delivery, security, and measurement.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and Google’s SDK data disclosures.

3. Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve the site and maintain security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, signup forms, or tracking cookies to this website. Its images and styles are hosted with the site. Following an external link or using your email application is subject to the relevant service provider’s practices.

4. Why information is used and who receives it

We use local game data to provide game functionality and your settings, and information you send us to provide support, address problems, and respond to requests. Google processes advertising and consent data as described above. GitHub processes website request information as the hosting provider. Our email provider processes messages needed to deliver and store correspondence.

We do not sell your local save or upload it to an advertising service. Advertising services may share information for advertising and measurement as described in their own policies; the legal meaning of “sale” or “sharing” can differ by jurisdiction.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests such as support and security, legal obligations, and consent where required for advertising or related storage. You can withdraw consent through the available privacy controls without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data: retained on your device until you clear the app’s storage or remove it, subject to any operating-system backup or restore settings. Clearing storage resets your progress and preferences. On Android, use the device’s app settings for Covelora to clear storage; menu names vary by device.
  • Backups: removing the app may not remove an existing platform backup. Manage those copies through your device or cloud-backup provider.
  • Support correspondence: retained only for as long as needed to resolve and follow up on your request, handle related issues, and satisfy applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. If information must be retained for a lawful reason, we will explain that reason where permitted.
  • Provider-held data: Google, GitHub, and your platform provider apply their own retention rules. Clearing Covelora’s storage does not necessarily delete data already processed by those providers. Their policies and privacy tools explain the available controls.

There is no Covelora account to delete in the current version. To request deletion of information you have sent directly to Afsanora, email covelora@afsanora.com. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete a local save that has not been sent to us.

6. Your privacy rights and controls

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. Contact us using the address below. We may need proportionate information to verify your request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

You can review your Android advertising-ID controls in your device settings, including reset or deletion options where available. Use the game’s Privacy control when displayed to revisit advertising choices. For information held independently by Google or GitHub, you can also use their privacy-request tools.

7. Age and children’s privacy

Covelora is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Players who are minors should involve a parent or guardian as required by local law. A minimum intended age of 13 does not replace any consent or age-related protections required in a player’s country.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. Google states that data transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Providers may process information in countries other than your own; their privacy policies describe the safeguards and transfer arrangements they use.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Covelora or its services change. We will publish the revised policy here and update the date above. If a change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide it. New features such as accounts, cloud saving, or real-money purchases will require corresponding updates before their introduction.
